What is a COC?

A Certificate of Compliance (COC) confirms that an electrical installation meets the required standards. It is commonly needed when selling a property and is often requested for rentals and insurance.

My power keeps tripping. What should I do?

Switch off appliances and note what was running when it tripped, then call us. Repeated tripping can point to a faulty appliance, an overloaded circuit or a wiring fault, so it should be checked.

My geyser has no hot water. Can you help?

Yes. We check the power supply, thermostat, element and wiring to find out why it isn't heating.
